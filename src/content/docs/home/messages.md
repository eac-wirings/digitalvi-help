---
title: Messages
description: Read notifications sent to you by DigitalVI or your administrators in Studio.
---

**Where:** Home → Messages (`/notifications`)

Messages is your Studio inbox. It holds notifications sent to your client, such as maintenance notices, release
announcements or messages from your administrator.

![Messages page](/screenshots/messages.png)

## Find a message

Use the **Received messages** panel:

| Field | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| Subject | Show only messages whose subject contains this text. |

Click **Filter** to apply it and **Clear** to reset.

## List of messages

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| From | Who sent the message. |
| Subject | The message title. Click a row to read it. |
| Date | When it was sent. Click the column header to sort. |

## Permissions

- **View customer notifications** lets a user read messages.
- **Manage customer notifications** and **Send messages** let a user create and send them.
