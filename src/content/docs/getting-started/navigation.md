## Signing in

Open your Studio address (for example `https://dev.digitalvi.app`) and sign in with your **username or email**
and **password**.

- **Remember me** keeps you signed in on this browser.
- **Forgot Password?** emails you a link to set a new password.
- **Microsoft (for internal staff only)** is single sign-on for DigitalVI staff. Customers should use their
  username and password.

:::tip
If an administrator has turned on one-time passwords (OTP) for your account, you'll be asked for a code from
your authenticator app after entering your password.
:::

## Your profile menu

Click your name in the top-right corner (shown as *Your Name (Client)*):

- **Profile**: change your name, email, password and two-factor settings.
- **Provide feedback**: send a comment or bug report straight to the DigitalVI team.
- **Logout**: sign out of Studio.

## Pin a page to your dashboard

Most pages have a **heart (♥)** icon in the top bar: *Pin this page to dashboard*. Click it, give the shortcut a
**Name** and click **Save**. The page then appears as a shortcut on your [Dashboard](/home/dashboard/). This is
handy for a player or playlist you check every day.

## Common controls

- **Filter panels**: most list pages have a filter box at the top. Fill in any fields and click **Filter**.
  Click **Clear** to reset it.
- **Details / Edit**: buttons on each row open that item.
- **Actions ▾**: on detail pages, the *Actions* menu next to the page title holds everything you can do with
  that item (edit, delete, schedules and so on).
- **Delete confirmation**: deleting always asks you to confirm first.
