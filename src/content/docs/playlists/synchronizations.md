---
title: Synchronizations
description: Automatically keep a playlist in sync with a Google Drive file or folder.
---

**Where:** Playlists → Synchronizations (`/synchronizations`)

A **synchronization** keeps a playlist up to date with a folder or file in **Google Drive**. Add, replace or
remove files in Drive, and Studio updates the playlist for you. Your team can update screens without signing
in to Studio.

![Synchronizations](/screenshots/synchronizations.png)

## Connect Google Drive

1. Click **Log in to Google Drive account**.
2. Sign in to Google and allow DigitalVI to read your files.
3. Choose the file or folder to sync and the target playlist.

## List of synchronizations

Filter by **Source file / folder** or **Target playlist**.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Source | The Google Drive file or folder. |
| Target playlist | The playlist that is updated. |
| Sync frequency | How often Studio checks for changes. |
| Last sync | When it last checked. |

:::tip
Google Docs, Sheets and Slides are converted to PDF for playback. To control margins and page size, see
*Google Docs convert settings* in [Player settings](/reference/player-settings/#file-synchronization).
:::

## Permissions needed

**Manage synchronizations**.
